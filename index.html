Winky’s Solitaires

Four classic solitaire games. Free. No ads. No accounts. Works offline.

Winky’s Solitaires brings four solitaire variations to your iPhone and iPad, plus a relaxed-rules option for a gentler game. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to buy — the four games are free, and the game never connects to the internet.

  • Relaxed-rules option
  • For iPhone and iPad
  • No ads, no accounts, no internet needed
