Support

Need help with Winky’s Solitaires? Email us at support@sunstormproductions.com. Tell us what happened, what device you are using (iPhone or iPad), and which version of iOS it is running, and we will get back to you.

Quick answers

  • Does the game need the internet? No. Winky’s Solitaires works entirely offline.
  • Do I need an account? No. There is no sign-in and no account of any kind.
  • Are there ads? No. The game has no ads.
  • Does it cost anything? No. The four games are free.
  • Something looks wrong in a game. Try closing the app completely and opening it again. If the problem is still there, email us with a description of what you were doing when it happened.
