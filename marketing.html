Winky’s Solitaires

Four classic solitaire games for iPhone and iPad. Free. No ads. No accounts. Works offline.

What you get

Winky’s Solitaires includes four solitaire variations in one app, plus a relaxed-rules option for a gentler game when you just want to unwind. Pick a game, shuffle up, and play — that’s all there is to it.

  • Four solitaire variations
  • Relaxed-rules option
  • For iPhone and iPad

Nothing in the way

There are no ads, no accounts, and nothing to buy in version 1 — the four games are free. The game never connects to the internet and collects no data at all, so it works anywhere, even with no signal.

  • No ads
  • No accounts or sign-up
  • Collects no data
  • Works offline
