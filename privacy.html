Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026

Winky’s Solitaires is made by Sunstorm Productions. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: it collects nothing.

Data we collect

None. Winky’s Solitaires collects no personal data and no usage data. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. The app has no internet connection and sends nothing anywhere.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress and settings are stored only on your own device, so the game can remember where you left off. That information never leaves your device.

In-app purchases

The current version of Winky’s Solitaires has no in-app purchases; the four games are free. A future version may add in-app purchases. Any purchase would be processed by Apple through the App Store, and we would receive no payment details from it.

Children

Because the app collects no data, there is no data to collect from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes what it collects, this policy will be updated here, and the new version will only apply from its effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email support@sunstormproductions.com.
