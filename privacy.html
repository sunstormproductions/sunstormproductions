Privacy Policy

Winky's Solitaires · Effective November 19, 2026

Winky's Solitaires is made by Sunstorm Productions (Robert S. Bettis), has four solitaire games, is free with no ads, no account, no sign-in, and a relaxed-rules option.

This policy explains what the app does and doesn't do with your information. The short version: it collects nothing.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking tools.

What stays on your device

Your game in progress, settings and statistics are stored only on your iPhone or iPad. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app removes them.

Apple

If you have chosen to share diagnostics and usage data with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may give us anonymous, aggregated crash and usage reports. We cannot identify you from them.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, for example if optional purchases are added in a future version, we will post the new version on this page and update the date above.

Contact

Questions? Email support@sunstormproductions.com.
